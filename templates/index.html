How it works

A modular training pipeline feeds a Flask app that serves predictions.

  1. Data ingestion

    Loads the raw dataset and creates an 80/20 train–test split.

  2. Transformation

    Imputes missing values, one-hot encodes categories and scales numeric features.

  3. Model training

    Tunes 7 regressors with GridSearchCV and keeps the best by R² score.

  4. Prediction

    The saved preprocessor and model score new inputs from the web form.
